What we stand for

Four pillars of
our organization

ذ

Spiritual Gatherings

Weekly Halqah-e-Zikr, Khafi Zikr, and Mehfil-e-Zikr sessions centred on Zikrullah, Qur'an recitation, Naat Khawani and collective du'a.

خ

Community Service

Food bank support, clothing distribution, and direct assistance to families and individuals.

ن

Nikah Services

Shariah-compliant nikah and financial assistance for families provided.

ع

Islamic Education

Imam Mahdi Online School offers foundational Islamic learning, Tafseer, Seerah, prayer guidance, and spiritual development for all.
