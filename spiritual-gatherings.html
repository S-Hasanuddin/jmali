Spiritual gatherings

Hearts gathered
in remembrance of Allah SWT

Every week, our community comes together in an atmosphere of peace and devotion to Zikrullah through various forms of zikr.

These gatherings aim to revive hearts, build unity, and create a space where seekers can grow spiritually in a warm and welcoming environment.

ح

Halqah-e-Zikr

A weekly circle of remembrance held every Thursday night, dedicated to collective Tilawat, Naat Khawani, zikr, khatam-e-khawjgan, spiritual reflection, and strengthening the heart through the blessings of Shab-e-Jumma.

Every Thursday Night
خ

Khafi Zikr

A practice of Qalbi Zikrullah, inward remembrance that nurtures inner tranquility and deepens one’s connection with Allah Azzawajal.

Every Friday Night
م

Mehfil-e-Zikr

A weekly gathering filled with soulful recitation, collective Zikrullah, Naat Khawani, dua, and spiritual companionship that uplifts the entire community.

Weekly — see schedule
