Imam Mahdi Online School

Knowledge rooted
in faith

Imam Mahdi Online School is a dedicated platform offering authentic Islamic education for both men, women and children, designed to strengthen foundational knowledge in Deenand nurture spiritual growth.

Core Areas of Learning

Offered Teachings

Structured programmes covering the essential pillars of Islamic knowledge — from foundational belief to Quranic understanding.

Essentials of Islam

Understanding the basic beliefs and practices that form the foundation of a Muslim's life — the five pillars, articles of faith, and core obligations.

01

Wudu & Namaz

Step-by-step guidance on purification and the five daily prayers, following the Sunnah with correct method, conditions, and inner mindfulness.

02

Fasting (Sawm)

Learning the rulings, virtues, and profound spiritual benefits of fasting — with focus on Ramadan, intention, and the inner dimensions of abstention.

03

Seerat un Nabi ﷺ

Exploring the blessed life, noble character, and divine mission of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — drawing lessons for modern Muslim life from his example.

04

Tafseer-e-Quran

Weekly sessions exploring the meanings, context, lessons, and timeless wisdom of the Holy Qur'an — verse by verse, with scholarly guidance.
